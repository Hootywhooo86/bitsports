## bitsports 0.5.0 — your teams first, every game a deep dive

- **Three tabs.** **Home** is only your teams: their games today, when they play next, and their news underneath. **Sports** has every game in the sports you watch, one league at a time (NHL, NFL, NBA, MLB, WNBA, college football, MLS, Premier League), with a live count on each. **Settings** is where everything you can change lives.
- **Pick teams by tapping.** Choose a league, tap your teams (up to 4 per sport), tap again to drop one. "Other" still takes any team in any sport for news.
- **LED jumbotron.** Your lead game in lit dot-matrix pixels at the top of Home, in team colours. Tap it to open the game.
- **Game screen, in depth.** Four tabs:
  - **Game**: who's on the ice right now drawn on a pixel rink with jersey numbers, goals by period, a shot map you can filter by team and period, every goal with clips, three stars. Before puck drop: last-10 form as pixel blocks, season stats head to head, players to watch, likely goalies, arena, TV and officials.
  - **Lineups**: forwards, defence and goalies with tonight's stats (tap a player for more), a dot on whoever's on the ice, the starting goalie, scratches and coach. Before the lineup posts, the season leaders.
  - **Plays**: live play-by-play, newest first.
  - **Stats**: team stats as pixel bars in each team's colours, plus each period with its AI recap.
- **Other leagues** show records, and tap to open leaders, TV, venue and what's happening now (down and distance, outs and runners).
- **Fix: "Couldn't reach the NHL feed."** The app kept checking scores while it was in the background, where Android cuts its network, and after one miss it waited 5 minutes to try again. Now it checks only while it's open, refreshes the moment you come back, retries a dropped request twice within seconds, and while it has recent scores just says "reconnecting" instead of the warning.

Install: download the APK below and open it. It installs over the previous version. Your teams and settings carry over.
